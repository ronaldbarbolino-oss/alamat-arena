Frequently asked questions

Is Alamat Arena free to play?

Yes. Alamat Arena is free. You can play right away in your browser or install the free Android app.

What kind of game is Alamat Arena?

It is a MOBA, a multiplayer online battle arena like Dota or Mobile Legends. Two teams fight across three lanes to destroy the enemy base.

Who are the heroes?

The heroes come from Philippine mythology and folklore: the Tikbalang, Kapre, Aswang, Manananggal, White Lady, Sirena, Sarimanok, Bungisngis and many more.

Can I play with friends online?

Yes. Create an online room and share it with friends for 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 or 5v5 matches, or use Quick Match.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open the game in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. It plays in landscape automatically.

Is there an Android app?

Yes. You can download the Android APK from this page or play directly in Chrome.
