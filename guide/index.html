How to play Alamat Arena

A beginner's guide to the Filipino mythology MOBA: goals, controls, lanes, jungle and items.

🎯 The goal

Two teams, the Sentinel and the Scourge, fight across three lanes: Top, Mid and Bot. Destroy the enemy towers one by one, then break their core to win.

🕹️ Controls on phone

Move with the joystick on the left. Tap ATTACK to hit the nearest enemy, and tap your skills on the right. Drag a skill to aim it, and slide back to cancel. Pinch to zoom.

⌨️ Controls on PC

Right-click to move or attack. Use Q, E and R for skills, B to recall to base, and the mouse wheel to zoom.

🌫️ Fog of war

You only see what your heroes, minions and towers can see. Enemies in the dark are hidden, so watch the minimap and stay with your team.

Your first match, step by step

  1. Pick a hero. New players can start with a tank like Kapre or a marksman like Amihan.
  2. Go to a lane and stay behind your minions. Last-hit enemy minions for gold.
  3. Buy items with your gold. Boots first, then items that fit your role.
  4. Farm the jungle. Boars and sigbin give extra gold and experience.
  5. Fight together. Use your ultimate in team fights and push towers after you win a fight.
  6. Take down the Bakunawa in the river for a big advantage, then end the game at the enemy core.

Hero roles

🛡️ Tank

Lots of health. Starts fights and protects the team.

⚔️ Fighter

Strong in close combat with good damage and health.

🗡️ Assassin

Fast and deadly. Picks off weak enemies.

🔮 Mage

Spells with big area damage and crowd control.

🏹 Marksman

Ranged attacker who deals steady damage late in the game.

✨ Support

Heals, shields and helps the team win fights.
