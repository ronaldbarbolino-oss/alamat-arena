ARENA

Sentinel vs Scourge, 5 vs 5 across three lanes. Pick a hero, destroy the towers, and bring down the enemy Ancient before they destroy yours.

Phone controls: left thumb = joystick to move · right buttons = ATTACK (hold) and skills · pinch with two fingers to zoom. Play in landscape.
PC controls: right-click or click the ground to move, an enemy to attack · drag the map or push the mouse to the screen edge to move the camera · mouse wheel or +/− zoom · Q E R skills · B recall · WASD move · Space attack · U quick-buy · P shop · I stats · T auto attack · F center camera · Esc pause.
Auto attack: your hero automatically hits nearby enemies while standing still. Jungle: trees block movement; defeat the Wild Boar, Sigbin and Bakunawa for gold. Items: every hero has a recommended build, use the gold quick-buy button. The ultimate (R) unlocks at Level 4. Each lane has 3 towers: outer, inner and base gate, before the Ancient.
